# Locus — Naming & Product Language

## Brand Direction

**Locus** is the umbrella brand.

The name is intentionally broad, calm, premium, and not tied to a specific sport such as cycling, running, or hiking.

The game and exploration mechanics are expressed through product areas and sub-names rather than forcing the main brand name to explain everything.

This allows Locus to grow into a broader outdoor exploration platform over time.

---

## Core Product Areas

### Explore

Free exploration of the world.

Use cases:
- Discover nearby locations
- Find collectibles
- Explore regions
- See undiscovered places
- Discover new targets during or after an activity

Possible product language:
- Explore
- Discover
- Nearby
- Unfound
- Discovered
- Next target

---

### Quests

Structured objectives made up of multiple locations, activities, or goals.

Examples:
- Visit all castles in Karlsruhe
- Discover 10 viewpoints
- Complete all lakes in a region
- Ride through 7 historic landmarks
- Finish a themed collection

Example:

**Locus Quest**

> Complete a themed journey.

A Quest can contain multiple objectives and track progress over time.

Example structure:

```text
Quest: Castles of Karlsruhe

Progress: 3 / 8

✓ Karlsruhe Palace
✓ Gottesaue Palace
✓ Favorite Palace
